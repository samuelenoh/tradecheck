    How liquidation is calculated

    Isolated margin, one-way mode. side = +1 for long, −1 for short. These follow each exchange's published help-center formulas.

    Binance USDⓈ-M: LP = (margin + maintenance amount − side × qty × entry) ÷ (qty × (MMR − side)) Bybit USDT perpetual: LP = entry − side × (initial margin − maintenance margin) ÷ qty, with maintenance margin = notional × MMR − deduction OKX USDT-margined: LP = (margin − side × qty × entry) ÷ (qty × (MMR + fee rate − side))
